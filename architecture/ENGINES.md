# Engines

| Engine | Primary responsibility | First output |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Gather and evaluate information | Evidence-backed research item |
| Academy | Convert information into reusable knowledge | Knowledge object / lesson |
| Opportunity | Discover and rank economic opportunities | Opportunity dossier |
| Innovation | Generate and test new concepts | Innovation proposal |
| Build | Turn approved concepts into working assets | Build artifact |
| Business | Operate approved activities | Operating task/result |
| Revenue | Monetize and optimize business engines | Revenue experiment/report |
| Learning | Measure outcomes and extract lessons | Learning record |

Each engine has an explicit input/output contract and must remain independently testable.
