# Governor

The Governor is AURELIX's orchestration and policy-enforcement core.

## Responsibilities
- maintain strategic objectives;
- route work to engines and agents;
- evaluate proposals;
- enforce authorization policies;
- detect conflicts and missing evidence;
- escalate protected actions;
- maintain decision state;
- produce audit-friendly decision records.

## Non-responsibilities
The Governor does not own capital, redefine the Constitution, or bypass security controls.

## Decision record
A significant decision should record:
- decision ID;
- objective;
- proposed action;
- evidence;
- alternatives;
- policy checks;
- risk;
- authorization requirement;
- decision;
- executor;
- outcome;
- follow-up.
