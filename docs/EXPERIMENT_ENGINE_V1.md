# AURELIX Experiment Engine V1

Experiments are the controlled bridge between an approved opportunity and learning.

## Rules

- Every experiment has an objective and measurable success metric.
- Every experiment has an explicit sandbox boundary.
- Every experiment has a declared budget, including zero-budget experiments.
- The experiment service does not execute arbitrary code.
- Production access is not implied by experiment approval.
- Financial spend remains subject to Owner Approval, Execution Gate and Treasury.
- Results must feed the Learning/Academy loop.

```text
Opportunity
    ↓
Experiment proposal
    ↓
Governor / approval
    ↓
Sandbox
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Result
    ↓
Learning
```
