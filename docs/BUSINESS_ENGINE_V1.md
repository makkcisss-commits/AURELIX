# AURELIX Business Engine V1

The Business Engine represents the private company's revenue-producing activities without granting those activities autonomous financial authority.

## Activity model

Each activity has:

- identity and description
- channel (SaaS, service, content, product, etc.)
- lifecycle stage
- monthly revenue
- monthly cost
- monthly margin

## Lifecycle

```text
IDEA
 ↓
VALIDATING
 ↓
ACTIVE
 ↓
PAUSED / CLOSED
```

## Governance

The Business Engine can measure and recommend. It cannot independently authorize spending, payments, production changes or external commitments.

```text
Research → Opportunity → Experiment → Business Activity → Revenue
                                      ↓
                                   Learning
```

All material spending continues through Owner Approval, Governor, Execution Gate and Treasury.
