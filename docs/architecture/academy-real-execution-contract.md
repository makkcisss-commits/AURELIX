# Academy real-execution contract

AURELIX Academy is complete only when a StudyObjective is consumed by a real execution path.

Required lifecycle:

1. A capability gap creates a StudyObjective.
2. Academy claims the objective and moves it to ACTIVE.
3. Research and/or bounded practice produces traceable Evidence tied to the objective.
4. An Experiment is created and actually executed by the Runtime experiment runner.
5. The measured result is recorded; no synthetic success is allowed.
6. ContinuousIntelligence evaluates the evidence and records PASS/FAIL.
7. A Capability may be validated only from a passing evaluation with referenced evidence.
8. Only the Governor may authorize operational execution.
9. A validated capability triggers durable resume of the original execution_id.
10. A failed/inconclusive evaluation leaves the original mission blocked and returns the objective to learning.

The Academy must not merely publish Knowledge. Publishing a summary is downstream of the real learning lifecycle and cannot substitute for evidence, experiment execution, evaluation, or capability validation.
