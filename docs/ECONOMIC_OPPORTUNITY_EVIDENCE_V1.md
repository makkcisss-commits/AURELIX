# Economic Opportunity Evidence V1

## Purpose

Separate three states that must never be conflated:

1. **Opportunity detected** — an economic hypothesis exists.
2. **Opportunity qualified** — required claims are supported by evidence.
3. **Revenue realized** — an authorized activity produced an observed revenue record.

## Required claims

An opportunity entering the revenue pipeline must have evidence for:

- `demand`
- `monetization_path`
- `source_reality`

Each claim must be supported with confidence of at least `0.6`. Missing or low-confidence claims keep the opportunity unqualified. Contradictory evidence marks the qualification as conflicted.

Qualification is **not authorization**. Governor approval remains the authority boundary for execution.

## Revenue boundary

A qualified and Governor-approved/recommended opportunity may become a validating revenue source. Estimated revenue remains an estimate until `RevenueEngine` records an observation. Only positive observed revenue activates a source.

This prevents the system from treating forecasts, scores, or agent assertions as realized income.
